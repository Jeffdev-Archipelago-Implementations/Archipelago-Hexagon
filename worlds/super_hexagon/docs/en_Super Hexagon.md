# Super Hexagon

## What does randomization do to this game?

Super Hexagon doesn't really randomize, as much as just lock levels. You receive level unlocks that allow you to play individual levels, and unlock your goal level by beating a specified number of levels and getting a specified amount of Hexagon Fragments.

## What is the goal of Super Hexagon in Archipelago?

Beating your goal level with the set survival time (default 60 seconds)

## What Super Hexagon items can appear in other players worlds?

There are unlocks for every level (except Hexagon, which you start with), as well as a "H E X A G O N" item that is just a silly nonsense filler item, and Hexagon Fragments if enabled which are your macguffin goal level unlock item.

## What does another world's item look like in Super Hexagon?

At present, new items obtained are not really shown within the game. This is something I'd like to change in the future, but currently, you can view items in the Super Hexagon Client, and use Universal Tracker if its installed within the Super Hexagon Client as well to see what you have available to use. Universal Tracker is fully supported, and works without a yaml.
